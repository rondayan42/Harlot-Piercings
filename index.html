The Ritual

The Sacred Ordinances of Healing

  • The First Fortnight: The wound is fresh, an open gate. Cleanse it twice daily with sterile saline — no alchemical compounds, no herbal tinctures. Pure salt and pure water.
  • Guard the Relic: The jewelry is sacred. Do not rotate, twist, or fidget with it. Your hands are profane until proven otherwise — touch only with freshly washed fingers.
  • Shun the Waters: Avoid submersion in lakes, pools, and baths for a minimum of four weeks. Standing water is a medieval plague unto fresh piercings.
  • The Body Will Rebel: Swelling, warmth, and clear discharge are the body's natural siege response. This is expected. Pus, fever, and expanding redness are not — seek counsel immediately.
  • Sleep as a Knight Sleeps: On your back, away from the wound. A travel pillow becomes your shield against nocturnal irritation.
  • Patience is the Final Trial: Full healing takes months, sometimes a year. Do not downsize, change, or remove jewelry before clearing it with your practitioner. The flesh remembers impatience.
